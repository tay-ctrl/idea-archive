# Idea Archive — Setup Guide

Your app lives at: **`https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/idea-archive/`**
(Once the app is deployed, replace YOUR-USERNAME with your GitHub username — the exact link will be given to you at deploy time.)

---

## 1. Install the app on your iPhone

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone (it must be Safari, not Chrome).
2. Type the app link (above) into the address bar and open it.
3. Wait for the page to fully load. You should see the blue "Idea Archive" screen.
4. Tap the **Share button** — the square with an arrow pointing up, at the bottom middle of Safari.
5. Scroll down the share menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
6. You'll see the blue lightbulb icon and the name "Idea Archive". Tap **Add** (top right).
7. The app now has its own icon on your home screen. From now on, always open it from that icon — not from Safari.

## 2. Check that it works offline (airplane-mode test)

1. Open the app once from its home screen icon (this makes sure everything is saved to your phone).
2. Close the app.
3. Turn on **Airplane Mode** (swipe down from the top-right corner and tap the airplane).
4. Open the app from its home screen icon again.
5. It should load and work exactly as before — add a test idea to be sure.
6. Turn Airplane Mode off. Done — the app works with no internet.

## 3. Make a backup

Your ideas are stored only on your phone, so make a backup now and then (for example, once a week or after adding important ideas).

1. Open the app and tap **Settings** (bottom right).
2. Tap **"Backup all data"**.
3. Your phone's share sheet appears. Tap **"Save to Files"** and pick a folder
   (choose **iCloud Drive** if you want the backup safe even if you lose the phone).
4. Tap **Save**. That's it — the file is named like `idea-archive-backup-2026-07-07.json`.

## 4. Restore from a backup

1. Open the app and tap **Settings**.
2. Tap **"Restore from backup"**.
3. Your Files app opens — find and tap the backup file you saved earlier.
4. The app shows you the **date of the backup** and how many ideas are inside.
5. Read the warning: restoring **replaces** everything currently in the app.
6. Tap **"Replace my data with this backup"** to confirm, or **Cancel** to keep what you have.

If you pick the wrong file (or a damaged one), the app will refuse it and your current data stays untouched.

## 5. Export ideas as a document

1. Go to the **Ideas** tab.
2. Tap **"Export"** next to a category name to export just that category,
   or **"Export all"** at the top to export everything.
3. The share sheet appears — save the `.md` file to Files, or share it anywhere.
   (`.md` is a plain text file — it opens in any notes or text app.)

## 6. Light and dark mode

Tap the **sun/moon button** in the top right corner to switch. The app remembers your choice.

## 7. Updating the app later

If the app is ever improved, here is how you get the new version:

1. Make a **backup first** (Settings → Backup all data) — just to be safe.
2. Open the app **with internet on** (Wi-Fi or data).
3. Close the app fully: swipe up from the bottom and hold, then swipe the app away.
4. Open it again. The new version downloads in the background.
5. Close and reopen once more — you're now on the new version.
   Your ideas are never touched by an update.

## Good to know

- **No account, no cloud**: everything stays on your phone. Nobody else can see your ideas.
- **Don't delete the app icon** unless you have a backup — deleting the app deletes its data.
- If your phone runs very low on storage, iOS can in rare cases clear app data — one more reason to keep a backup in iCloud Files.
